# Zeer product illustrations

The repository should read as a working developer product: an editorial opening,
a legible data path, a short installation path and scoped evidence. Use the
existing Zeer mark unchanged. These are original explanatory compositions, not
website recordings or live execution captures.

## Craft contract

Before the transition, a viewer sees source data and the developer's selection
or model. After it, the viewer sees exactly what the next application layer can
consume. One record or connection owns attention. Every scene settles into an
indefinitely readable final diagram; nothing important depends on catching a frame.

## Tokens

- Ink `#0b1010`, panel `#111b16`, elevated panel `#1a281f`, stroke `#34473b`.
- Cream `#f3f1e7`, sage `#a0b09f`, lime `#c6ff42`.
- Original SVG mark from `apps/web/src/app/icon.svg`.
- Manrope headings, IBM Plex Mono fields. Self-hosted fonts and their OFL files.
- Hero canvas 1280 × 720. Docs canvases 1040 × 600, composed from a 1280 × 720
  stage with proportional scaling and centered vertical space.
- 64px stage gutter; 56–66px titles; 36px product names; 22–28px code/data;
  18px footnotes. Short labels carry the story at narrow image widths.
- Panel radius 20px, fine borders, a restrained upper-edge light and soft shadow.

## Motion

144 frames at 30 fps: 4.8 seconds. Cubic ease `.22, 1, .36, 1`; short 12–22-frame
entries, sequential connections, one small packet per link, at most 1.016 camera
scale. Complete the explanation before frame 105, then hold. GIF exports play
once and stop; no infinite motion, flashing or simulated throughput counters.
PNG posters show the complete story and serve reduced-motion readers. Motion follows dependency order and preserves a shared spatial anchor; all
state is frame-derived. The brand and compositions are Zeer's own.

## Compositions and primitives

- Overview: Zcash source → Prep selection → Chef model/persistence → application API.
- Prep: an explicit public block range and two selected fields become a projected response.
- Chef: Prep → handlers → PostgreSQL, then an application-defined query and related values.
- Primitives: wordmark, product label, field chip, product node, connector, query
  panel and result panel. States are prepared, selected and settled.
- Values use the public testnet acceptance sample. The private scanner is optional;
  its key remains in the operator's environment. No animation claims fresh execution,
  universal speed, consensus verification or automatic settlement.

## Delivery

Commit the small final GIF/PNG assets and this reproducible source. Keep render
intermediates in `.cache/`. Do not publish the separate working demo-video folder,
raw Audiobooks exports, credentials or screenshots of authenticated services.
